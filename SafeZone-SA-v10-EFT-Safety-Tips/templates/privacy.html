PRIVACY

Your information

SafeZone SA is designed to collect as little information as practical. Your Trusted Circle is private to your account. Location is requested only when a feature needs it and your browser permits it.

Emergency location

When you activate an emergency event, the app may record the latitude and longitude your device provides. It is used to create the emergency event and a location link for your trusted contacts.

Community reports

Reports are stored for moderation. They are not automatically treated as fact. Admins can verify or reject reports before they are presented as verified community information.

Important

Do not use SafeZone as a replacement for emergency services. In an immediate emergency, call the appropriate emergency service directly.
