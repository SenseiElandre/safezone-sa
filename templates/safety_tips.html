SAFETY GUIDE

Stay safer

In immediate danger

Move to a safer location if you can do so without increasing the danger, and contact the appropriate emergency service.

When travelling

Tell someone you trust where you are going, keep your phone accessible, and avoid isolated areas when possible.

If confronted by criminals

Prioritise your life and avoid unnecessary confrontation. Follow official emergency guidance and seek help when safe.

These are general safety reminders, not professional emergency advice.
